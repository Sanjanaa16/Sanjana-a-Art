The Artist

Painting with precision and soul

I'm Dr. Sanjana Rajesh Upadhyay, the artist behind Denartist. I am a Dentist and currently pursuing an MTech in Medical Device Innovation, and my work carries both technical discipline and emotional storytelling.

I am also the founder of RayoScan AI and Dreams Over Degrees. From portraits and devotional compositions to acrylic experiments and mirror mosaic, I create art that is personal, grounded, and made with love.
